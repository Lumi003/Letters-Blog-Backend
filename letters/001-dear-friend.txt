Dear friend,

I've been meaning to write this for some time now. There's a particular kind of quiet that settles over a house in early morning — before the kettle has been filled, before the light has decided what colour it wants to be. I find I do my best thinking then.

It occurred to me, standing at the window with yesterday's cold coffee, that we rarely say the things we mean to say. We orbit around them, leaving ellipses where full stops should be.

This letter is my attempt to remedy that. A small act of courage, really. Nothing more than words on a page — but then, what else have we ever had?

With warmth,
The Author
