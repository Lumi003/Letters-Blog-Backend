Red-Balloon. If I said I have a letter for you, Would you read it?

---

I found it on a Tuesday. A **red balloon**, half-deflated, caught in the iron fence outside the post office.

Someone had tied a *small note* to its string. The handwriting was careful, deliberate — the kind you only use when you mean every word.

> "For whoever finds this — I hope your day is warmer than mine."

I stood there for a long time.

## What we leave behind

We cast things out into the world — <span class="red">words</span>, <span class="gold">gestures</span>, <span class="blue">letters tied to balloons</span> — and we rarely know where they land.

But sometimes they land exactly where they should.

I kept the note. I let the balloon go.
