A note on small things,

The cat knocked a glass off the table this morning. She watched it fall with the detached academic interest of someone who has read extensively about gravity but never seen it demonstrated.

I've been thinking about that look. The glass fell anyway. It didn't matter that she understood it was coming.

Some things are like that. You can see them approaching from a long way off. You understand the physics perfectly. And still, when they arrive, you're surprised by the sound they make.

I swept up the pieces. The cat sat nearby, satisfied, having contributed something to the morning.

Small things. I'm writing about small things now, because the large things are too large and the medium things are too medium. Small things fit in a letter. They travel well.
