On the subject of rain,

I have been trying to write about the rain for three weeks. Every attempt ends in the bin. The trouble with rain is that everyone has already felt it and everyone has already written it, and yet when you're standing in it — genuinely in it, not metaphorically — it feels entirely new.

I walked home through a downpour on Tuesday. No umbrella. I had one, I just chose not to open it. There is something clarifying about being thoroughly, completely wet. All the small anxieties you carry in your collar and your cuffs wash clean away.

I arrived home looking like I'd crossed a river. I made tea. I felt enormously alive.

Maybe that's all I wanted to say.
